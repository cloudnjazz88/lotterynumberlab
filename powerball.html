Powerball rules, odds and prize tiers

Powerball draws five white balls from 1–69 and one Powerball from 1–26. Order does not matter, so the number of possible tickets is C(69, 5) × 26 = 292,201,338 — the jackpot odds. Drawings are held mondays, wednesdays & saturdays at 10:59 p.m. ET. Power Play is an optional $1 add-on that multiplies non-jackpot prizes by 2X–10X (the $1,000,000 tier is capped at $2,000,000).

What this generator does — and what it cannot do

The generator weights each ball by how often it has been drawn, how long it has been missing, its momentum over the last 60 drawings and which balls it tends to appear with, then shapes each line so its sum, odd/even split, low/high split and decade spread look like a real drawing. That makes the output statistically typical of past winners. It does not make it more likely to win: the 292,201,338 possible tickets are, and remain, equally likely.

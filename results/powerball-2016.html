Powerball's most frequent number in 2016 was 23, drawn 16 times — in 15.2% of the year's drawings. Every number from 1 to 69 came up at least once, with the quietest appearing just 1 time (45). The most common Powerball was 3, with 7 appearances.

None of this predicts anything. A number that led one year has no better chance the next, and the ranking reshuffles completely from year to year — which is exactly what testing the full record against simulated fair draws shows.

How to interpret these 2016 figures

Powerball completed 105 drawings in 2016 with a white-ball average of 176.7 — almost exactly the long-run matrix mean of 176.9. Even when a year lands on the average, individual drawings still swing from the low 76s to the high 275s.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 7.6 hits per number, 2016's leader board looks busy. 2 white balls tied at 16 appearances (23, 64). The quietest count was 1, shared by number 45. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 33 drawings (31.4%). Drawings with at least one consecutive white-ball pair landed 27.6% of the time, in line with the matrix-wide 26.3%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Powerball in 2016 was 3 (7×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

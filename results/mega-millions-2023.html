How to interpret these 2023 figures

Mega Millions completed 104 drawings in 2023 with a white-ball average of 174.9 — almost exactly the long-run matrix mean of 174.5. Even when a year lands on the average, individual drawings still swing from the low 67s to the high 276s.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 7.4 hits per number, 2023's leader board looks busy. White ball 29 led with 15 appearances (14.4% of drawings). The quietest count was 1, shared by number 56. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 3 odd / 2 even — 38 drawings (36.5%). Drawings with at least one consecutive white-ball pair landed 25.0% of the time, in line with the matrix-wide 25.8%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2023 was 18 (8×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

How to interpret these 2020 figures

Mega Millions held 104 drawings in 2020, and the white-ball sum averaged 176.2, a little above the long-run matrix average of 174.5. Year-to-year wiggles of a few points are the normal footprint of independent draws, not a signal to chase.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 7.4 hits per number, 2020's leader board looks busy. 2 white balls tied at 12 appearances (15, 44). The quietest count was 2, shared by number 65. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 30 drawings (28.8%). Consecutive pairs showed up in 32 drawings (30.8%), a bit above the matrix-wide 25.8%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2020 was 22 (8×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

Most and least drawn numbers of 2020

Powerball's most frequent number in 2020 was 36, drawn 14 times — in 13.3% of the year's drawings. Every number from 1 to 69 came up at least once, with the quietest appearing just 3 times (26, 28, 66). The most common Powerball was 18, with 13 appearances.

None of this predicts anything. A number that led one year has no better chance the next, and the ranking reshuffles completely from year to year — which is exactly what testing the full record against simulated fair draws shows.

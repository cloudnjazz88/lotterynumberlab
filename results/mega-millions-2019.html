Most and least drawn numbers of 2019

With 105 drawings and five balls each, every number "should" appear about 7.5 times if the machine is fair. The spread below is what fair randomness actually looks like over a single year — a leader several appearances clear of the field, and a tail of numbers that barely showed up.

Mega Millions's most frequent number in 2019 was 48, drawn 17 times — in 16.2% of the year's drawings. Every number from 1 to 70 came up at least once, with the quietest appearing just 2 times (1, 51, 69). The most common Mega Ball was 10, with 8 appearances.

None of this predicts anything. A number that led one year has no better chance the next, and the ranking reshuffles completely from year to year — which is exactly what testing the full record against simulated fair draws shows.

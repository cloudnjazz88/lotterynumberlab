How to interpret these 2025 figures

2025 is a past calendar year relative to the snapshot reference date October 1, 2026. This page describes the 104 bundled Mega Millions drawings from January 3, 2025 through December 30, 2025. That span is what the archive holds. It is not a claim that every official drawing of the year was captured.

The five white balls in these 2025 drawings averaged 170.9. The observed average across the bundled archive of 931 drawings from October 31, 2017 through September 29, 2026 is 174.4. The 2025 average is 3.5 points below that observed archive average. The archive average is the mean of those recorded drawings, not a theoretical mean of the ball matrix.

A uniform mathematical expectation for one specified white ball in this year's record is about 7.4 appearances (104 × 5 / 70). That expectation is not the observed archive average. 2 white balls tied at 14 appearances (10, 31). The lowest count was 2, shared by 3 numbers. The tally describes these drawings only and does not change the odds of a later drawing.

The most common odd/even split in 2025 was 3 odd / 2 even, in 35 drawings (33.7%). Consecutive white-ball pairs appeared in 32 of 104 drawings (30.8%). The observed rate in the bundled archive of 931 drawings is 25.9%. These are counts, not a test of whether the difference is ordinary.

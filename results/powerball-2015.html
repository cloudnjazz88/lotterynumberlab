How to interpret these 2015 figures

2015 is a short archive year for Powerball: 25 drawings from 2015-10-07 through 2015-12-30. Small samples swing hard — a number can lead the board after a handful of appearances and then vanish from the next year's ranking. Treat every figure on this page as a description of what already happened.

With only 25 drawings, many of the 69 white balls never showed up. 8 white balls tied at 4 appearances (16, 20, 30, 32, 40, 47, 56, 68). 12 numbers from 1–69 never appeared at all in 2015. Gaps that large are normal in a short window; they are not "due" numbers for the next drawing.

The most common odd/even split was 3 odd / 2 even — 10 drawings (40.0%). Drawings with at least one consecutive white-ball pair landed 28.0% of the time, in line with the matrix-wide 26.3%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Powerball in 2015 was 1 (3×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

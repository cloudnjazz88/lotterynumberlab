How to interpret these 2018 figures

2018 is a past calendar year relative to the snapshot reference date October 8, 2026. This page describes the 104 bundled Mega Millions drawings from January 2, 2018 through December 28, 2018. That span is what the archive holds. It is not a claim that every official drawing of the year was captured.

The five white balls in these 2018 drawings averaged 172.1. The observed average across the bundled archive of 933 drawings from October 31, 2017 through October 6, 2026 is 174.5. The 2018 average is 2.4 points below that observed archive average. The archive average is the mean of those recorded drawings, not a theoretical mean of the ball matrix.

A uniform mathematical expectation for one specified white ball in this year's record is about 7.4 appearances (104 × 5 / 70). That expectation is not the observed archive average. White ball 1 led with 16 appearances (15.4% of these drawings). The lowest count was 2, shared by number 36. The tally describes these drawings only and does not change the odds of a later drawing.

The most common odd/even split in 2018 was 3 odd / 2 even, in 38 drawings (36.5%). Consecutive white-ball pairs appeared in 27 of 104 drawings (26.0%). The observed rate in the bundled archive of 933 drawings is 25.9%. These are counts, not a test of whether the difference is ordinary.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2018 was 23, drawn 8 times. Each Mega Millions drawing is an independent trial. Hot, cold, and overdue labels summarize records; they do not change the odds on the next ticket. Verify any real ticket with your state lottery.

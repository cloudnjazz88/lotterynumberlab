How to interpret these 2025 figures

Powerball held 157 drawings in 2025, and the white-ball sum averaged 175.2, a little below the long-run matrix average of 176.9. Year-to-year wiggles of a few points are the normal footprint of independent draws, not a signal to chase.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 11.4 hits per number, 2025's leader board looks busy. White ball 28 led with 24 appearances (15.3% of drawings). The quietest count was 6, shared by 4 numbers. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 55 drawings (35.0%). Consecutive pairs showed up in 36 drawings (22.9%), a bit under the matrix-wide 26.3%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Powerball in 2025 was 20 (12×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

How to interpret these 2025 figures

2025 is a past calendar year relative to the snapshot reference date October 7, 2026. This page describes the 157 bundled Powerball drawings from January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2025. That span is what the archive holds. It is not a claim that every official drawing of the year was captured.

The five white balls in these 2025 drawings averaged 175.2. The observed average across the bundled archive of 1416 drawings from October 7, 2015 through October 5, 2026 is 176.8. The 2025 average is 1.7 points below that observed archive average. The archive average is the mean of those recorded drawings, not a theoretical mean of the ball matrix.

A uniform mathematical expectation for one specified white ball in this year's record is about 11.4 appearances (157 × 5 / 69). That expectation is not the observed archive average. White ball 28 led with 24 appearances (15.3% of these drawings). The lowest count was 6, shared by 4 numbers. The tally describes these drawings only and does not change the odds of a later drawing.

The most common odd/even split in 2025 was 2 odd / 3 even, in 55 drawings (35.0%). Consecutive white-ball pairs appeared in 36 of 157 drawings (22.9%). The observed rate in the bundled archive of 1416 drawings is 26.2%. These are counts, not a test of whether the difference is ordinary.

The most frequent Powerball in 2025 was 20, drawn 12 times. Each Powerball drawing is an independent trial. Hot, cold, and overdue labels summarize records; they do not change the odds on the next ticket. Verify any real ticket with your state lottery.

How to interpret these 2022 figures

Mega Millions ran 104 drawings in 2022. The five white balls averaged 166.8 — well below this matrix's long-run mean of 174.4. A year-long tilt that large still sits inside ordinary sampling noise for a fair drum; it is not evidence the machine "favored" high or low totals.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 7.4 hits per number, 2022's leader board looks busy. White ball 38 led with 16 appearances (15.4% of drawings). The quietest count was 1, shared by number 49. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 40 drawings (38.5%). Drawings with at least one consecutive white-ball pair landed 28.8% of the time, in line with the matrix-wide 25.9%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2022 was 24 (7×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

With 104 drawings and five balls each, every number "should" appear about 7.4 times if the machine is fair. The spread below is what fair randomness actually looks like over a single year — a leader several appearances clear of the field, and a tail of numbers that barely showed up.

Mega Millions's most frequent number in 2022 was 38, drawn 16 times — in 15.4% of the year's drawings. Every number from 1 to 70 came up at least once, with the quietest appearing just 1 time (49). The most common Mega Ball was 24, with 7 appearances.

None of this predicts anything. A number that led one year has no better chance the next, and the ranking reshuffles completely from year to year — which is exactly what testing the full record against simulated fair draws shows.

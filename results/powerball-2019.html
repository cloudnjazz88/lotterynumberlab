How to interpret these 2019 figures

Powerball ran 104 drawings in 2019. The five white balls averaged 170.0 — well below this matrix's long-run mean of 176.9. A year-long tilt that large still sits inside ordinary sampling noise for a fair drum; it is not evidence the machine "favored" high or low totals.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 7.5 hits per number, 2019's leader board looks busy. White ball 14 led with 14 appearances (13.5% of drawings). The quietest count was 3, shared by 3 numbers. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 34 drawings (32.7%). Consecutive pairs showed up in 24 drawings (23.1%), a bit under the matrix-wide 26.3%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Powerball in 2019 was 21 (8×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

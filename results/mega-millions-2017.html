Most and least drawn numbers of 2017

With 18 drawings and five balls each, every number "should" appear about 1.3 times if the machine is fair. The spread below is what fair randomness actually looks like over a single year — a leader several appearances clear of the field, and a tail of numbers that barely showed up.

Mega Millions's most frequent number in 2017 was 42, drawn 5 times — in 27.8% of the year's drawings. 18 numbers were never drawn at all in this period (2, 5, 9, 11, 13, 19, 29, 32, 34, 35, 43, 45, …). The most common Mega Ball was 3, with 2 appearances.

None of this predicts anything. A number that led one year has no better chance the next, and the ranking reshuffles completely from year to year — which is exactly what testing the full record against simulated fair draws shows.

How to interpret these 2017 figures

2017 is a short archive year for Mega Millions: 18 drawings from 2017-10-31 through 2017-12-29. Small samples swing hard — a number can lead the board after a handful of appearances and then vanish from the next year's ranking. Treat every figure on this page as a description of what already happened.

With only 18 drawings, many of the 70 white balls never showed up. White ball 42 led with 5 appearances (27.8% of drawings). 18 numbers from 1–70 never appeared at all in 2017. Gaps that large are normal in a short window; they are not "due" numbers for the next drawing.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 8 drawings (44.4%). Consecutive pairs showed up in 6 drawings (33.3%), a bit above the matrix-wide 25.8%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2017 was 3 (2×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

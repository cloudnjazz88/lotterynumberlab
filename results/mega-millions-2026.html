How to interpret these 2026 figures

2026 is still in progress in this archive: 77 Mega Millions drawings so far, from 2026-01-02 to 2026-09-25. Mid-year totals move as new results land; nothing here is a projection of how the year will finish.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 5.5 hits per number, 2026's leader board looks busy. White ball 30 led with 11 appearances (14.3% of drawings). The quietest count was 1, shared by number 28. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 26 drawings (33.8%). Consecutive pairs showed up in 11 drawings (14.3%), a bit under the matrix-wide 25.8%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2026 was 12 (10×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.

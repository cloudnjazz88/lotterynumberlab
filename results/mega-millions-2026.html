How to interpret these 2026 figures

2026 is year-to-date coverage in this snapshot. The reference date is October 7, 2026. These 80 Mega Millions drawings run from January 2, 2026 through October 6, 2026. Drawings later in 2026 are outside this snapshot.

The five white balls in these 2026 drawings averaged 185.2. The observed average across the bundled archive of 933 drawings from October 31, 2017 through October 6, 2026 is 174.5. The 2026 average is 10.7 points above that observed archive average. The archive average is the mean of those recorded drawings, not a theoretical mean of the ball matrix.

A uniform mathematical expectation for one specified white ball in this year's record is about 5.7 appearances (80 × 5 / 70). That expectation is not the observed archive average. White ball 30 led with 11 appearances (13.8% of these drawings). The lowest count was 1, shared by number 28. The tally describes these drawings only and does not change the odds of a later drawing.

The most common odd/even split in 2026 was 2 odd / 3 even, in 27 drawings (33.8%). Consecutive white-ball pairs appeared in 13 of 80 drawings (16.3%). The observed rate in the bundled archive of 933 drawings is 25.9%. These are counts, not a test of whether the difference is ordinary.

The most frequent Mega Ball in 2026 was 12, drawn 10 times. Each Mega Millions drawing is an independent trial. Hot, cold, and overdue labels summarize records; they do not change the odds on the next ticket. Verify any real ticket with your state lottery.

How to interpret these 2026 figures

Powerball held 114 drawings in 2026, and the white-ball sum averaged 179.1, a little above the long-run matrix average of 176.9. Year-to-year wiggles of a few points are the normal footprint of independent draws, not a signal to chase.

Against a fair-share baseline of about 8.3 hits per number, 2026's leader board looks busy. White ball 64 led with 16 appearances (14.0% of drawings). The quietest count was 1, shared by number 1. Leaders reshuffle every year when you re-rank the same matrix — which is what you expect when each drawing is an independent event, not a memory of the last one.

The most common odd/even split was 2 odd / 3 even — 35 drawings (30.7%). Drawings with at least one consecutive white-ball pair landed 23.7% of the time, in line with the matrix-wide 26.3%. Neither pattern is a playbook; both are side effects of how combinations are counted, and each new draw resets the slate.

The most frequent Powerball in 2026 was 14 (9×). Bonus-ball streaks look meaningful on a year page and still carry no edge: the next drawing does not owe anyone a repeat or a make-up hit. This archive is for looking back — verify any ticket with your state lottery, and never treat hot, cold, or overdue lists as a way to beat the odds.
